# New states: evidence notes (fetched Nov 24, 2026)

Everything below comes from official state agency pages I actually fetched. Quotes are word for word from each page. Volunteer Fire Assistance (VFA/VFC) programs use federal USDA Forest Service money, but the state forestry agency runs them and takes the applications, the same setup as the NY DEC VFC example in the task. I counted them as state-run.

## Virginia
1. **DOF Dry Fire Hydrant Grant, 2027-03-31.** dof.virginia.gov/.../dry-hydrants/: "Deadline: March 31 each year"; "Program support is available at the request of Virginia fire departments who secure approval from local landowners."; "The program provides 100% of the funding necessary to cover the expenses of new installations and repairs." The DOF Financial Assistance Programs table says the same: "Dry Hydrant Grant Program | DOF | Fire Departments | ... | March 31 Annually".
2. **DOF VFA Grant, 2027-03-31.** The DOF Financial Assistance Programs table (https://dof.virginia.gov/financial-assistance-programs/) lists: "Volunteer Fire Assistance Grant Program | Fire Departments | March 31 Annually". The VFA page shows "Application period closed." (that is the 2026 cycle) and "Virginia Department of Forestry is not allowed to provide full or partial grant funding for any one piece of equipment over $5,000 in cost." Caveat: the date comes from the DOF summary table, not the VFA page itself.

## Wisconsin
3. **DNR Forest Fire Protection, 2027-07-01.** "July 1 | Last day to submit applications"; "Applications accepted May 1 through July 1."; "Application materials will be available on this page prior to May 1, 2027."; "Maximum grant: $10,000 ($20,000 total project costs)".
4. **DSPS Youth Volunteer Firefighter Training, tbd.** "Typically, grant announcement and deadline occurs February to March of each year."; "Minimum request is $500, and maximum is $25,000." The 2026 round was the fifth (DSPS news release, Jan 30, 2026). No 2027 date has been posted.

## Missouri
5. **MDC VFA Cost-Share, tbd.** "Every year, Missouri fire departments serving communities of less than 10,000 can apply for a 50 percent reimbursement grant ... with a maximum reimbursement of $3,000." The page gives no application dates.
6. **DFS VFPA Workers' Comp Insurance Grant, rolling.** "Disbursements of available funding will occur on a first come, first serve basis upon approval of any submitted application while funds are available."; "eligible VFPA agencies may apply ... for up to $2,000". Caveat: only membership- or subscriber-funded Volunteer Fire Protection Associations qualify, not tax-supported fire districts. Funding is appropriated each fiscal year (FY26: $200,000).

## Iowa
7. **DNR VFA, tbd.** "The 2026 Volunteer Fire Assistance Grant Application Packet ... Grant applications are due November 2, 2026." That deadline has passed. The program runs every year (the DNR Forestry Fire page lists 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 rounds). The next date is not published.
8. **DIAL LFPEMS Grant, tbd.** "The grant application period for 2026-2027 is now closed."; "Any local fire protection service provider or local emergency medical service provider in the state of Iowa who complies with NFIRS or EMS reporting requirements of the state may apply". The program was created by Iowa Code 10A.519(7)(b).

## West Virginia
9. **State Fire Commission VFD Equipment & Training Grant, tbd.** From firemarshal.wv.gov media/39770: "As funding allows, the grant program is expected to become available for VFD in fiscal year 2027. Please check back to this page in early August of 2026 for a link to apply. All applications will be completed online via www.wvoasis.gov VSS portal."; "Grant awards and disbursements will vary but typically, will not exceed $10,000." The Fire Department Services page lists "FY2027 Grant Funding for Volunteer Departments" but gives no dates. Caveat: the rule (87CSR10) says it "shall terminate ... on August 1, 2027", though an amendment was filed with the WV Secretary of State on 6/29/2026.

## Vermont
10. **FPR Wildland Fire Gear Grant, tbd.** The 2026 cycle ran "Grant Opens - May 15, 2026 ... Grant Closes - June 1, 2026"; "A reimbursement of 50% of total costs, up to $3000". It is funded every year with federal VFC money. No 2027 dates are posted.

## New Hampshire
11. **DFL VFA, 2027-02-01.** "Grant applications are sent to Forest Fire Wardens and Fire Chiefs each November and have a submission deadline of February 1st."; "fire suppression organizations in communities of less than 10,000 in population."

## Georgia
12. **GFC NFP/VFA 50/50, rolling.** GFC announcement letter (gatrees.org PDF, 2024 cycle): "This will be an on-going grant application period. Fire Departments will be able to apply for the grant throughout the year. Applications will be reviewed for approvals on a quarterly basis."; "Fire Departments may qualify for reimbursement of up to 50% of $10,000.00 or $5,000.00 maximum per government entity." The current RFD page says: "Cooperating Fire Departments can submit documents to receive 50/50 grants ... Applications must be submitted through online system." It gives no deadline. Caveat: the rolling language comes from the letter that opened Oct 1, 2024. A GFC blog post from Feb 2026 says the program was recently made "faster and more flexible."

## South Carolina
13. **V-SAFE, tbd.** SC State Fire newsletter, Jan 2026: "The 'Notice of Intent' to apply for a grant is now open through January 15, 2026 ... must be at least 50 percent volunteer"; "The grant application period will open late February for 45 days." It runs every year (recipient lists for 2019 through 2026). No 2027 notice-of-intent date is posted.
14. **SCFC VFA, tbd.** Program page: "Receive up to $7,500"; "In 2026, the VFA program will move to fully digital/ electronic." The 2025 packet said "The application deadline is December 31, 2025" and "Maximum Award: Grants are available for up to $5,000." No 2026/27 deadline is posted. The two pages give different maximums ($7,500 vs $5,000).

## Indiana
15. **DNR VFA, 2027-03-01.** "This is a 50/50 grant, and the application deadline is March 1 of each grant year." The DNR Fire Program page says "Maximum Award $5,000".
16. **IFPSA Local Training Support Grant, tbd.** "FY2026 Timeline - Application Period: June 1-30, 2026"; the FY2026 NOFO says "$10,000 capped award". Grants have been offered every year since FY2022. Eligibility is narrow: departments must own training centers in the Hub-and-Spoke program, have MOUs, or help with Academy courses.

## Dropped in skeptic review
- **VDFP Live Fire Training Structure Grant (VA).** Next postmark date is Jan 1, 2027, but it is "Open to Virginia localities," not fire departments.
- **VDFP Aid to Localities / Fire Programs Fund (VA).** A formula payment to localities that file an annual report. It is not a competitive grant and has no deadline posted.
- **Missouri DPS Volunteer Fire Department Grant (up to $15,000).** It was "first-ever" in SFY2026. The page says the deadline "Will close when eligible applications have been received", and there is no evidence it will run again.
- **WV Division of Forestry VFA (80/20, up to $2,000, Jan 31, 2026).** I found it only on the state firefighters' association site and a third-party grant database, never on an official WVDOF page.
- **Vermont Rural Fire Protection (dry hydrant) grants.** Run by the Vermont Association of Conservation Districts, not a state agency.
- **Vermont Technical Rescue Grant (H.935).** A bill that depends on funding. No program page.
- **Iowa LOSAP.** For municipalities and individual volunteers, not fire departments; its 2026 window has closed.
- **Wisconsin 2% Fire Dues.** A formula payment to municipalities. Departments don't apply for it.
