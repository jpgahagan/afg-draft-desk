# AFG Draft Desk: owner checklist (under 30 minutes)

## A. Send the outreach (about 10 minutes)
1. Open Gmail drafts in the wagl mailbox and search: `in:drafts "Free AFG grant draft for"` (25 drafts).
2. For each, change From to a non-wagl AFG Draft Desk address you control (not wagl, not COMPÁS), then send it yourself.
4. If anyone replies "no thanks", forward it to the task so the address goes on the suppression list.

## B. FEMA GO Grant Writer account (about 10 minutes, needed before the $795 tier)
1. Go to https://go.fema.gov and choose "Sign in with Login.gov".
2. Create a Login.gov account with the AFG Draft Desk email and set up multi-factor authentication.
3. Back in FEMA GO, enter your first and last name to finish the profile.
4. For each $795 customer: the chief (the department's Authorized Organization Representative, or AOR) adds that same email as Grant Writer. Accept the invite while signed in with that email.
5. Only the AOR can sign and submit. You fill in the application, and they review and submit.

Sources: FEMA GO startup guide https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema-go_startup-guide.pdf ; AFG account creation guide https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_go-afg-account-creation-user-guide.pdf

## C. Payout setup (when buyers are waiting)
Set up a free payment link yourself (for example Stripe Payment Links). The task never handles bank details.
Grant-writer fees can be a pre-award cost up to $1,500 if they are competitively sourced and listed in Request Details. Contingency fees are not allowed. https://apply07.grants.gov/grantsws/rest/opportunity/att/download/352178
