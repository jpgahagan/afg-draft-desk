Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Backdraft Grants is a trade name used by Joseph Gahagan, 1390 Chain Bridge Rd #10140, McLean, VA 22101 ("Backdraft Grants," "we," "us"). This policy explains what information we collect through backdraftgrants.com and our services, how we use it, and the choices you have. Questions: hello@backdraftgrants.com.

The short version

  • The free draft tool runs in your browser. The draft text and the answers you tap are saved only on your device. We do not receive them.
  • If you reserve a plan, we keep your department name, email and any optional details you add, so we can contact you when FEMA opens the next round.
  • We count page visits and button clicks without cookies and without storing your IP address in our own records.
  • We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Reservations. Department name and email address (required). Optional: state, department type, what you plan to request, and a note. We also record when you reserved and which version of our Terms of Service you accepted.
  • Email. If you write to us, we receive your message, your email address and anything you include.
  • Paid services, when offered. To finish narratives or prepare an application, we will need information about your department, such as budget figures, call volume, equipment condition, vendor quotes and contact details for your officers. We will ask for this only after you choose a paid service.

Information the free tool uses

  • Department search. When you search for your department, your search words and state go to our server so it can return matching departments. Our application does not save these searches. Our hosting providers may keep them in standard request logs (see below).
  • Your draft and answers. The draft is built in your browser. Your answers and the draft are saved in your browser's local storage for up to 120 days, so you can pick up where you left off. They are not sent to us. You can clear them with "Start over" or by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Email, Word and copy buttons. "Email it to myself" opens your own email app; we do not see or send that email. The Word file is created on your device.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage events. When a page loads or you use certain buttons (for example, opening the draft tool or starting the reservation form), your browser sends us a short record: the type of event, the time, the page, a short tag showing which page or link brought you (such as "email_ny" or "state_maine"), and a random number created for that page visit. The random number is not stored on your device and changes every time a page loads. We use these records to count visits and see which pages and links are useful.
  • No cookies from us. Our site does not set cookies. It uses your browser's storage only for the saved draft described above and a short-lived note that prevents repeated page reloads after we update the site.
  • Hosting and network logs. Like nearly all websites, our service providers receive your IP address, browser type and the pages you request when your browser connects to them. They may keep this in their own logs under their own policies.
  • Fonts. Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address and browser details to Google when a page loads. Google describes its handling in the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Public data about fire departments

The draft tool is prefilled with public information about departments and communities: the U.S. Fire Administration's National Fire Department Registry (department name, location, type, stations and staffing as reported by departments) and U.S. Census Bureau population, poverty and income estimates. This is information about organizations and counties, not about individual visitors. If your department's registry entry is wrong, the U.S. Fire Administration maintains the registry and can correct it at the source.

How we use information

  • To run the free tool and return department search results.
  • To hold your reservation and email you when FEMA publishes the next funding notice, with the intake form and the invoice for the plan you chose.
  • To provide paid services you ask for, and to communicate with you about them.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To keep the site secure, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations.

AI-assisted work

The free draft tool does not use artificial intelligence. It fills fixed templates with public data and your answers, in your browser.

For paid services, we use third-party AI writing tools (currently Perplexity) to help rewrite and organize narratives from the information you give us. A person reviews the work before it is delivered. When we use these tools, your department's information is processed by that provider under its own terms and privacy policy. Grant narratives are about your department, not individuals, so please send only the department facts the application needs. We will never ask for Social Security numbers or personal financial information. If you prefer that your information not be processed with AI tools, tell us before you buy.

Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed:

  • Perplexity hosts the website and its database, and provides AI tools used for paid work.
  • Cloudflare delivers backdraftgrants.com and protects it from attacks.
  • Google provides our email (Google Workspace) and the fonts used on the site. Your browser requests the fonts directly from Google, so Google receives your IP address.
  • GitHub stores the website's source code. It does not hold your reservation or draft.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or others' safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of the business (in which case this policy would continue to apply to the information). We do not sell personal information and do not share it for targeted advertising.

Fire departments are often public bodies. Messages and documents you send us may be subject to your state's public records laws on your side; we treat them as confidential on ours.

How long we keep information

We have not set a fixed retention period. We keep reservation details and emails until you ask us to delete them, or until we no longer need them for the purposes above, whichever comes first. Usage event records contain no names or contact details and may be kept for as long as they are useful for counting visits. Information in your browser stays there for up to 120 days unless you clear it sooner.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Reservation records can only be read with a private access key held by Backdraft Grants. No method of storing or sending information is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send passwords, bank details or Social Security numbers by email. We will never ask for your FEMA GO, Login.gov or SAM.gov password.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction and deletion. Email hello@backdraftgrants.com from the address you used, and we will tell you what we hold, correct it or delete it. We may need to confirm the request came from you.
  • Cancel a reservation. Email us at any time. There is no charge.
  • Stop emails. Reply "no thanks" or "unsubscribe" to any email from us, or write to us. We honor opt-outs promptly and within 10 business days.
  • Your browser data. Use "Start over" in the draft tool, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

Depending on where you live, state privacy laws may give you additional rights, such as the right to know, correct or delete personal information, or to appeal a decision about a request. You can make any of these requests by email even if a particular law does not apply to us. If we can't fulfill a request, we will explain why, and you can ask us to reconsider by replying.

Browsers' "Do Not Track" signals are not used by our site because we do not track visitors across websites.

Commercial email

We email fire departments individually, at addresses the department publishes for official contact, about grant programs relevant to them. Every message identifies Backdraft Grants and our mailing address and tells you how to opt out. We do not buy email lists.

Children

Our services are for fire departments and the adults who represent them. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use information you already gave us, we will tell you by email before it takes effect.
