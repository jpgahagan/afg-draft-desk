      Questions

      Who writes the drafts?

      Drafts are written with AI from your intake answers and checked against FEMA's published narrative guidance and the current funding notice. Every number comes from you. Your department reviews and approves everything before it is submitted.

      Do you guarantee an award?

      No. Awards are competitive and decided by FEMA's review panels. We do not charge contingency fees, which FEMA does not allow as a grant cost.

      Can you submit the application for us?

      No one outside your department can. In FEMA GO, only your Authorized Organization Representative can sign and submit. With Full FEMA GO prep, you add us as your Grant Writer, we fill in the entire application, and your official reviews it, signs and submits in a few minutes.

      Do we have to disclose that we used you?

      Yes. FEMA asks applicants to identify anyone who helped prepare the application, paid or not. We give you the exact details to enter.

      What about SAFER, Fire Prevention and Safety, or state grants?

      The free draft tool covers AFG, SAFER and Fire Prevention and Safety, and both paid plans cover all three at the same prices. For state grants, see the deadline list above and mention the program in the form.
