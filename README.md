# Backdraft Grants

Flat-fee ($249) drafts of the four scored FEMA Assistance to Firefighters Grant (AFG) narratives for volunteer and combination fire departments: Financial Need, Project Description, Cost-Benefit and Statement of Effect.

- Live page and free narrative checker: https://backdraft.pplx.app/?src=github
- The checker counts characters against FEMA's 4,000-character limit and flags missing elements from FEMA's narrative guidance. It runs entirely in the browser.

## AFG prep checklist (free)
Gather these before the FY2026 window opens. The FY2025 window ran May 19 to June 22, 2026.
1. SAM.gov registration active, plus FEMA GO access for your authorized representative.
2. Annual operating budget with revenue sources and major expenses.
3. Call volume for the last 3 years, by type.
4. Age, condition and standard compliance (NFPA) of the items you want to replace.
5. At least two vendor quotes for every requested item.
6. Population served and whether it is under 20,000 (this affects cost share).

Sources: [Federal Register notice 2026-18419](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/10/2026-18419/assistance-to-firefighters-grant-program-fire-prevention-and-safety-grants), [FY2025 AFG funding notice](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/documents/fema_rsl_hmd_fy25-afg-nofo_05292026.pdf).
